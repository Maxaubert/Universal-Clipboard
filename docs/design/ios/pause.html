Pause and Resume: three ways

Each option plays one story in about 10 seconds: Max pauses, it stays paused for a moment, then he resumes. The wave is desktop A4 "Hush": logos fade to grey and green dots drain, Resume sends a green wave back and the dots fill again. Paused means opening the app does not take or give copies. After Resume the app takes the newest copy, so the line under the title rolls to it.
