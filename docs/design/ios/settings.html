Settings and devices: three ways

The iPhone has few settings: this iPhone's name, Sync images and the 25 MB limit, a tip for the iOS paste setting, How it works, the other devices, and Remove this iPhone. Updates come from the App Store, so About shows the version and no update button. T1 and T2 keep a separate Settings screen; T3 puts it all on Home and also shows the one-time "Updated" card after an App Store update.
