How to choose

  1. Each page is one choice. Its options are different versions of the same screens, side by side. Pick one per page.
  2. Each option plays its whole story and loops. Press Replay to see it again.
  3. Use the buttons at the top: Reduced motion, Dark Mode, Actual size.
  4. Reply with the ids you like, for example F1, W2, H1, X3, P1, E1, T2. Mixing parts is fine.

What carries over from the desktop

  • S1 One shape (setup), E1 Shake, then clear (code errors), H3 You are here (this device apart), A3 Ticker, A4 Hush.
  • 6 digits and a QR code, local network only. No copy history: the app shows facts, never content.
  • iPhone rules: a copy moves when you open the app or use the Share sheet. Updates come from the App Store.
  • Real iPhone paste prompts are not checked yet (spike S5).
