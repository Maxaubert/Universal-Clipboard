iPad Home and settings

These are the iPad versions of Max's picks H3 Pinned and T3 On Home, in landscape and portrait. They are not alternatives: check them, there is nothing to choose. Landscape is a split view: Home in the sidebar, the settings in the pane on the right. Portrait is one column with the settings under the devices. The iPad syncs like the iPhone: when you open the app or use the Share sheet.
