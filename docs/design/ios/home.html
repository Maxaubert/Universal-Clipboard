Home: three ways

Home is what you see each time you open the app, and opening it is the moment the iPhone syncs. Each story: the app opens, the newest copy from Max's PC arrives (arrow down), then the app reads what you copied on this iPhone and sends it (arrow up). The line under "Working" rolls to each copy and back to "3 devices connected", like desktop A3. Max's iPad is asleep. The screen shows facts about a copy, never the copy itself.
